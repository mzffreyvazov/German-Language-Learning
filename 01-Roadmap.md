# Roadmap

## Next lessons
### L001 — German Alphabet, Sounds, and Pronunciation
- type: skill
- status: planned
- description: Covers the German alphabet, vowel sounds (including umlauts ä, ö, ü), diphthongs (ei, ie, eu/äu), and unique consonants and clusters (ch, sch, sp, st, ß). Connects German phonology to familiar sounds in Azerbaijani, Turkish, and English to build an immediate phonological foundation for reading.

### L002 — Essential Greetings and Courtesies
- type: topic
- status: planned
- description: Introduces high-frequency formulaic expressions for greeting, parting, expressing gratitude, and polite interaction (hallo, guten Tag, danke, bitte, auf Wiedersehen). Establishes early confidence recognizing and pronouncing complete communicative expressions.

### L003 — Personal Pronouns & Present Tense of 'sein' and 'haben'
- type: grammar
- status: planned
- description: Introduces subject pronouns (ich, du, er/sie/es, wir, ihr, sie/Sie) and the present tense conjugations of the two vital auxiliary/linking verbs 'sein' (to be) and 'haben' (to have). Bridges English parallels (I am / I have) and provides the foundation for basic identity and possession statements.

### L004 — Basic Sentence Structure & Regular Present Tense Verbs
- type: grammar
- status: planned
- description: Explains regular present tense verb inflection endings (-e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en) with high-frequency verbs (kommen, wohnen, lernen, machen, trinken) and the fundamental Verb-Second (V2) rule in main declarative statements and simple inverted questions.

### L005 — Core Nouns, Gender & Definite/Indefinite Articles
- type: grammar
- status: planned
- description: Introduces the concept of three grammatical genders (masculine, feminine, neuter) with nominative definite (der, die, das) and indefinite (ein, eine, ein) articles, basic plural markers, and the universal capitalization of German nouns.

### G001 — Familiarization Gate
- type: gate
- status: planned
- description: Evaluates spelling-to-sound mastery, recognition of foundational vocabulary, basic reading comprehension across 2-3 short passages, and V2 word order recognition. Marks successful completion of the familiarization phase and transition to the loop phase.
- gate: G001
